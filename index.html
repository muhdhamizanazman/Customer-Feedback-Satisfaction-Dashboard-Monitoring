Project summary

  • Problem: feedback data is spread across thousands of rows, so it is hard to see where service or product quality is slipping.
  • Solution: a one-page Excel dashboard with KPI cards, charts, slicers and written insights, refreshed with a single VBA button.
  • My contribution: data cleaning, a new Age Group field, PivotTables, charts, slicers, macro, insight box and layout design.

What the dashboard includes

  • KPI cards: satisfaction score (85.28%), service quality (5.49/10), product quality (5.49/10) and total responses (38,444).
  • Status legend: a colour guide that tells the viewer whether a score is good, intermediate or critical.
  • Charts: service and product quality by country, customers by gender, customers by country, customers by age group, and satisfaction by loyalty level.
  • Slicers: Country, Age Group and Loyalty Level filters that update the whole dashboard.
  • Refresh button: a VBA macro that refreshes all PivotTables and charts in one click.
  • Insight box: short written findings so the viewer gets the message without studying every chart.

Project objective

Build a single, easy-to-read dashboard that lets managers monitor customer feedback and satisfaction at a glance, and quickly filter by country, age group and loyalty level.

Business questions answered

  • What is the overall satisfaction score, and is it good or critical?
  • Which countries lead or trail in service and product quality?
  • Where are most of our customers located?
  • Which age groups respond the most?
  • Do Gold, Silver and Bronze customers differ in satisfaction?

Success criteria

  • KPIs readable in under 5 seconds
  • All charts react to the slicers
  • One-click refresh with VBA
  • Clear status colours and written insights
  • Clean, consistent visual design

Target users

Customer experience managers, regional managers and the quality assurance team.

Project activities

Click each step to expand.

1Data acquisition
Downloaded the CSV from Kaggle and imported it into Excel with Power Query / Data → From Text/CSV.
2Data cleaning & validation
  • Checked for blanks, duplicates and wrong data types
  • Standardised category names (Country, LoyaltyLevel)
  • Converted the range into an Excel Table so PivotTables update automatically
3Feature engineering: Age Group
Created a new column that groups raw ages into three bands for easier analysis. 
=IF([@Age]<=30,"18-30",IF([@Age]<=50,"31-50","51-70"))
4PivotTables & calculations
  • Average service and product quality by country
  • Customer count by country, gender and age group
  • Average satisfaction by country and loyalty level
  • Overall KPIs: satisfaction %, service, product, total responses
5Charts & slicers
Built column, donut, line and clustered bar charts from the PivotTables, then linked Country, Age Group and Loyalty Level slicers to every pivot with Report Connections.
6VBA refresh button
A simple macro assigned to the REFRESH shape updates every PivotTable and chart in one click. 
Sub RefreshDashboard()
    ThisWorkbook.RefreshAll
    MsgBox "Dashboard refreshed!", vbInformation
End Sub
7Design & polish
  • Light grey canvas with white chart cards
  • Navy and orange colour palette used across charts, slicers and buttons
  • Colour-coded KPI cards, rounded to two decimals
  • Charts titled "Average ..." for accuracy, with customer counts sorted high to low
  • Status legend (good, intermediate, critical) beside the KPI cards
  • Written insight box added to the dashboard
8Review & next steps
  • Start chart axes at zero, or label zoomed axes clearly
  • Add targets for the service and product quality scores (out of 10)
  • Show the same country order across charts for easier comparison
  • Add a satisfaction trend chart if a date field is available

Key insights

France leads service quality (5.51) and UK trails (5.46), a gap of only 1.0%.
USA leads product quality (5.53); Germany is lowest (5.46).
USA has the most customers (7,762) and UK the fewest (7,628).
The happiest segment is Germany Bronze customers (85.76%); the lowest is Germany Gold (84.63%).
Overall satisfaction is 85.28%, which sits in the Good range (above 80%).
The 31-50 age group is the largest, and gender is evenly split 50/50.

Recommendations

  • Review service processes in the UK and share practices from France, the top performer.
  • Investigate why Germany Gold customers score lower than Germany Bronze, and run a targeted follow-up survey.
  • Set targets (for example 7/10) for service and product quality so those scores can be judged as good or bad, like the satisfaction KPI.
  • Collect response dates to track satisfaction trends over time.

Limitations

Scores are very similar across countries, age groups and gender (50/50), which is typical of synthetic Kaggle data. Differences of about 1% are small, so findings are best used to demonstrate the method rather than to draw real business conclusions.
